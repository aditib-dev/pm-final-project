Velocity: driver future-state journey

RouteLogic · Module 1 · Strategy: reclaim operational simplicity by stripping away legacy noise and making the tool the fastest for everyday work.

Persona
The frontline driver (composite of UXR-01, 08, 11, 12)
Role
An experienced delivery driver who completes dozens of stops a day, often in poor conditions and with their hands full.
Goal
Get through the route fast, with every delivery recorded correctly the first time.
Workaround
Texts the dispatcher instead of marking stops delivered in the app (UXR-01).

The four stages

  1. Start the route
    • User action: Taps "Start Route" from the home screen → the route begins immediately, with no hunting.
    • Internal state: Finds the daily action first → feels in control from the first minute.
    • Pain point addressed: Removes the buried button used 30 times a day. (UXR-11, BUG-2079)
  2. Work the route
    • User action: Opens the next stop straight from the stop list → no menu diving between deliveries.
    • Internal state: Knows where every core action lives → calm, even in week two.
    • Pain point addressed: Replaces menus inside menus → new drivers can find failed-delivery reporting. (UXR-08)
  3. Complete the stop
    • User action: Taps once at the doorstep → the stop is recorded, even with one hand full.
    • Internal state: Delivery done and recorded in one moment → no reason to text instead.
    • Pain point addressed: Replaces three taps across three screens, the top frontline complaint. (UXR-01, BUG-2055)
  4. Close the route
    • User action: Finishes with every stop logged in the app → nothing left to reconcile.
    • Internal state: Ends the shift actually done → no follow-up messages to dispatch.
    • Pain point addressed: Ends the texting workaround → completions stay in the platform the customer pays for. (UXR-10)

Competitive advantages over the manual workaround

  1. Fewer steps at the door: one tap replaces switching apps, naming the stop and sending a text.
  2. The record lives where it belongs: completion is logged against the stop, not buried in a message thread.
  3. No second person needed: dispatchers no longer read, interpret or re-enter drivers' texts.
Scope: This journey covers what Velocity changes. It does not address mid-route crashes, offline failure, photo upload failures or dashboard lag (BUG-2031, 2050, 2061, 2072), so a completion logged in stage 4 may still reach the dispatcher's board late.
